A LITTLE PLANNING GOES A LONG WAY

Every swap
is a decision.

Line up three or more matching circuit tiles to clear them. Longer chains pay more. Watch the move budget — a swap only counts when it makes a match.

01

Focus & select

← → ↑ ↓ move focus while the field is focused. Enter selects a tile; Enter again on an adjacent tile swaps. On touch, tap a tile, then tap an adjacent tile.

02

Chains & budgets

P or Esc pauses. Cleared tiles drop and refill, and falling tiles can chain into bigger scores. If no move remains, the board shuffles free of charge.

03

Keep it local

Optional: stores only your best score in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Uncheck to remove it.

Sound is optional and starts off.

Accessibility: keyboard controls, touch controls, reduced-motion support and readable status are included. Tiles differ by shape as well as color, and the focus indicator is a dashed outline with corner brackets, not color alone. This is a turn-based game with no time pressure.
