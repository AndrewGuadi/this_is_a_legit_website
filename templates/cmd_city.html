        KEEP THE LIGHTS ON

        A city needs a balance.

        Construction is paid once. Upkeep, debt payments and taxes are booked each game month.

        The confirmation shows borrowing terms before you accept. Finance can redeem an existing obligation early with enough treasury and an operating Town Hall.

        Tax rates

        Entering a sector rate sets all three wealth-class rates together and replaces individual class settings. View the current class rates in Residents & wealth. Empty zoning pays no tax.

        THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE POPULATION

        Residents & wealth.

        Homes have private cash, age groups, work, shopping needs and education progress. A home’s wealth is earned through sustained conditions; it is not the height of its building.

        These are household cohorts. Individual aging, births, deaths and named residents remain outside this milestone.

        Private household budgets

        Wages and arriving residents bring explicitly external private funds. Shopping pays merchants; it is not deposited into city treasury. Actual residential taxes are municipal receipts.

        A DESTINATION IS MORE THAN A RADIUS

        Shopping & education

        Purchases need a staffed shop, goods, private cash and an actual road route with remaining capacity. School and university places are finite; adult study requires twelve attended months. A missed month pauses progress.

        STAFF. REACH. COLLECT. RETURN.

        Services & dispatch.

        Vehicles travel connected roads to actual targets and return with their cargo. Busy vehicles and unmet cases persist when a road breaks or a facility closes.

        Vehicles move in discrete monthly steps. Symbols on the map show their saved road positions. Influence-area color does not mean a vehicle has arrived.

        Collection & waste

        Pickup moves material into cargo. A real return delivers it to finite storage or processing. Treatment and case resolution happen after admission, at staffed throughput.

          WHAT HAPPENS HERE STAYS SOMEWHERE

          A persistent environment.

          Air moves with the wind. Ground contamination and radiation can remain after a source closes or is demolished. Cleanup does finite monthly work.

          Emissions shown here are the opening-month activity forecast. Production, staffing and stocks may change later in the month. These are game indicators, not physical measurements.

          Water quality & treatment

          Contamination indicators run from 0 to 100. Filters remove 90% only from the share of actual delivered water they can staff and process; they create no extra water supply.

          Selected-site recovery

          This original scenario ruins a selected nuclear facility and contaminates a finite area. It requires confirmation and can affect residents. Inspect the site first; export a recovery copy if needed.

          New nuclear construction requires Utilities and Education departments plus a peak population of 5,000. The plant also consumes finite ore fuel.

          Air advection is a finite-cell model. Ground contamination, radiation and health are bounded indicators; water quality is weighted delivery rather than hydraulic pipe routing.

          THE PUBLIC GOOD

          Service funding

          Influence areas remain aggregate indicators. Services & dispatch shows actual road vehicles, pickups, deliveries and finite treatment. Funding alone does not teleport service to a covered tile.

                THE BIG IDEA

                Everything connects.

                Start with a road to the regional entrance. Zone homes, shops and industry beside connected roads. Add electricity, water, sewage and waste capacity. Then watch one month, inspect the results and decide what comes next.

                Understand the model

                Roads carry utilities in this build. Population, jobs, traffic, public-service coverage and environmental effects are simplified game systems, not real-world predictions. The inspector describes the rules at work.

                Keyboard controls and HTML tile descriptions support navigation. The visual map still has nonvisual accessibility limits.

                Feature-equivalence progress

                The target is a detailed feature-by-feature city simulator. This is a development milestone, not full feature equivalence. Curved roads, complete agent simulation, full regional multiplayer and expansion systems remain to be completed and verified.
