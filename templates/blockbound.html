YOUR FIRST WALK THROUGH FIRST LIGHT

Follow the glow.
Bring it home.

Three golden light shards are tucked around the harbor. Face a shard or resident and interact when the prompt appears. The northern beacon wakes only when all three shards are found.

01

Walk & look

W A S D move and step sideways. Arrow Up/Down moves; Arrow Left/Right turns. Touch controls work on smaller screens.

02

Meet & recover

E or Space talks to a nearby resident, collects a shard, or uses the beacon. P or Esc pauses.

03

Original by design

This compact city, its residents, and its artwork are original. It does not use a commercial ROM, copied map, or branded character art. Sound is optional and starts off unless you enabled it before.

Accessibility: keyboard controls, touch controls, reduced-motion support, text dialogue, and readable quest status are included. Navigating the 3D city requires vision; this game does not offer equivalent nonvisual play.
