THREE IN A ROW. ONE MORE ROUND.

Build the line.
Break theirs.

Every turn is a choice: add your own mark to an empty cell, or remove one opposing mark. A completed line earns a point and ends only that round—not the match.

01

Place or erase

Choose Place or Remove, then choose a cell. Switching actions is free; only a legal board move passes the turn.

02

Keep the match moving

Lines score one point. A full board without a line is a draw. Start the next round to keep playing with the same scores.

03

Break a repeated loop

If both players repeat their own exact action on the same cell for three consecutive turns each, the board resets automatically. The match score remains.

04

Keyboard and sound

Tab to the board, use arrow keys between cells, and press Enter or Space. Sound is optional and starts off. Audio begins only after your input. Only your sound preference can be stored in this browser; match moves are never saved or sent.

Accessibility: every cell is a native button with a text label, visible focus, and non-color X/O markers. Round results and loop resets are announced. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

BACK TO ZERO?

Reset the whole match?

This clears the board, both scores, the round count, and repeat history. Cancel to keep the current match exactly as it is.
