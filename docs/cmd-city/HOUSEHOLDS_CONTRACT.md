# CMD-03 household economy and education contract

Original household-cohort systems for the confirmed SimCity (2013) + Cities of Tomorrow comparison. This is a per-home aggregate model, not named individual agents or a calibrated real-world economy. Children, working-age adults and seniors are tracked; chronological aging, births and deaths are not simulated in this module.

## Integration API

- `createHouseholds(city,{month=city.month}={})`: fresh state for `city.households`; migration gives current occupants low-wealth cohorts, explicit external starting private cash, no retrospective education or attendance progress.
- `reconcileHouseholds(city)`: mutates only `city.households` to match actual occupied housing. Never changes a tile's occupancy, city cash, RNG or goods inventory.
- `householdReport(city,metrics)`: pure; virtually reconciles raw fixture/changed tile occupancy so displayed demographic counts are current without mutating the model.
- `advanceHouseholds(city,metrics)`: after growth/incident reconciliation and production, BEFORE municipal finance/current `city.month` increment. Processes `city.month+1` once, marks `households.lastMonth` ahead by one, collects actual private residential taxes and shopping costs, and consumes only actually sold goods. Core then increments city.month, making the state stable. Returns `{report,bookedTaxes,shoppingLoad,trips,tripsTruncated}`; core MUST book `bookedTaxes`, not recompute after wealth transitions. Repeating the same prefinance hook is inert.
- `validateHouseholds(value,city,{stable=true}={})`: returns true or throws. Stable saves require marker==city.month and exact cross-tile cohort counts. Transient validation allows an ahead-one marker or unreconciled occupancy, while still validating every record and financial identity.
- `householdAction(city,{type:'classTax',sector,wealth,value},metrics)`: returns `{ok,message}`. Primitive sector ID, numeric wealth 0–2 and rate 0–20; requires installed Finance plus an open, supplied gateway-connected Town Hall. Rejections preserve the whole city.
- `setSectorTaxes(city,sector,value)`: returns `{ok,message}` and sets all three class rates. This preserves the existing legacy sector-tax action's authorization; core retains its own validation and writes the legacy scalar for compatibility.
- `taxRevenue(city,metrics)`: pure next-month forecast `{residential,commercial,industrial,total}`. Residential forecast accounts for existing private cash, actual routed employment wages and arrears. `effectiveTaxRate(city,sector,metrics)` returns the population/occupied-unit weighted current rate, or the arithmetic class mean if there are no taxable units.

Metrics must supply 1,280-entry `homeEmployment` (ACTUAL routed employment, never more than the home's adults), `jobFill`, `access`, `landValue`, `coverage` and utility supply/demand/ratios. Optional `operationAccess` further restricts operating facilities. Education and shopping also compute real orthogonal road paths between endpoints; aggregate coverage is insufficient.

## State and accounting

`{version:1,lastMonth,homes:[null|record],shoppingLoad:[...N],wealthTaxes:{residential:[...3],commercial:[...3],industrial:[...3]},pending:{immigration,emigration,writtenOffTaxes},ledger:{...}}`.

Home records: wealth 0–2; children/adults/seniors; educated adults; private cash and tax arrears; satisfaction and unmet shopping; good/bad month streaks; fixed adult-training cohort (`trainees`) and attended-month progress (`study`); last employment, shopping demand/purchases, enrollment, wages, actual residential tax paid and spending. Residents are never added by this subsystem. Initial adults are floor(occupancy×0.6) and children floor(occupancy×0.25), with seniors as the remainder. Subsequent growth/shrink uses deterministic largest-remainder proportional apportionment of the home’s actual existing groups; it never ages one group into another. The exported cheap `householdWorkers(city,index)` returns actual persisted adults when counts match, otherwise the proportional virtual adult count without report/graph allocation.

A new resident brings 10,000 minor units of external private starting capital. Growth adds that declared source; departures remove proportionate private cash and write off proportionate uncollected tax debt. A new home begins low wealth; growth within an existing household retains its household class but brings only low-wealth starting capital. No private balances are deposited in the municipal treasury by reconciliation.

Monthly sequence: reconcile → external wage payments from actual routed employment → collect current residential taxes and earlier arrears up to private cash → actual shopping → school/university enrollment and adult study → satisfaction and at-most-one wealth transition → commit the private ledger and return booked municipal taxes.

Wages are an explicitly external private income source, not imaginary municipal income or a conserved firm payroll. Merchant spending is an explicit private-economy sink: actual goods and household cash leave their respective stocks, merchant receipts are reported, and no sales value is minted into city.cash. Firm cash balances and a complete macroeconomic money supply remain outside this milestone. Commercial/industrial taxes retain the existing aggregate firm-tax model and are recorded separately from household cash flows.

Private cash is capped at 1,000,000,000 minor units per home. Wage income above headroom is reported as capped/unpaid external income and never silently added or removed. Tax arrears are capped similarly; any uncollectible excess is explicitly recorded as written-off tax, never municipal income. The ledger must reconcile `openingCash + immigration - emigration + wages - residentialTaxes - spending == closingCash` and `openingArrears + taxDue - residentialTaxes - writtenOffTaxes == closingArrears`.

## Connected shopping and finite education

One retail bundle is requested per four residents (rounded up). Each purchase reserves one trip against every road tile on its actual shortest path, after the authoritative commuting `metrics.roadLoad`; a full or fractional-insufficient bottleneck blocks that purchase. Adaptive rerouting to a longer uncongested path is not implemented in this milestone. Each staffed, supplied, open commercial lot can sell four bundles per filled worker monthly, bounded jointly by shared city goods stock, the home's cash, and a real road path. Prices by shop land-value class are 700/1,000/1,500 minor units. Repeated one-bundle allocation rounds rotate their first household deterministically by month, rather than permanently favoring low tile indices. No RNG is consumed. Actual `shoppingLoad` counts every assigned bundle trip; at most 128 actual path samples are returned, with an explicit truncation flag. The full N-length array is persisted at `city.households.shoppingLoad` and exposed as a defensive copy at `householdReport(...).shopping.load`. Repeated prefinance hooks return the same saved loads, never zeros derived from missing path samples. The validator requires N bounded nonnegative integers. Reconciliation deliberately preserves this last-booked-month snapshot even after homes or roads are removed; overlays must label it historical rather than a new traffic prediction. Returned report/action load arrays never alias authoritative state.

A supplied funded connected school provides 60 child enrollment slots; a university provides 40 adult training slots. Modules and school funding scale these finite capacities, further bounded by actual filled staffing: eight school workers or twelve university workers for full capacity. Every enrollment needs a real road path. Coverage alone never graduates anyone. Adult training admits a fixed cohort and requires twelve attended months; missing capacity pauses progress. Newly admitted adults never inherit an older cohort's eleven-month credit. Children attend school but do not age into adults here.

## Wealth and taxes

Land value alone cannot change wealth. A promotion needs six sustained good months, actual employment, shopping fulfillment, educated adults, school access for children, supplied utilities and sufficient private cash per resident. Low→middle and middle→high have increasingly strict thresholds. Three bad months can reduce wealth by one class; no transition changes population. Conditions and thresholds are exported as original balancing constants.

Residential wealth is the home's economic cohort class. Commercial/industrial wealth classes are an explicitly aggregate land-value approximation used for class taxation; they are not demographic business agents. All three class rates default to 9, preserving uniform-tax arithmetic when private taxpayers can actually pay. Wealth transitions occur after the month's actual tax collection; booked receipts therefore retain the class used at collection, even if the next forecast changes.

## UI report

`{population,classes:[{wealth,label,people,homes,cash}],demographics:{children,adults,seniors,educatedAdults},budgets:{...ledger,currentCash,arrears},shopping:{wanted,bought,unmet,spent,load},education:{children,enrolledChildren,adults,educatedAdults,enrolledAdults,trainees},satisfaction,taxes:{rates,effective,forecast,booked,month},homes:[null|details]}`.

Each home detail includes current demographic/wealth/budget state, current tax rate, and last processed spending/enrollment. A pure report never changes RNG, enrollment, private balances, city stock or municipality receipts. Actual trips are returned from the monthly action, not fabricated by a render routine.

## Bounded route computation and equivalent optimization

The household route layer caches only gateway-connected road topology and BFS distance/predecessor arrays. Its signature includes geometry and every road's current active state (road type membership, enabled state and fire); topology changes invalidate it. The cache is a WeakMap keyed by city, with at most 256 distance/predecessor pairs per city. At the maximum 1,280 tiles, those Int16Array pairs use at most 1.25 MiB plus bounded graph/map overhead. No household body, wallet, employment, staffing, funding, tax rate, price, supply or road-capacity decision is retained in that cache. A road-class upgrade reads its current capacity when assigning each shopping trip.

Retail choices are sorted by the same shortest distance, price and tile-index tie rules, but only generated when a household can actually shop. Rejected capacity candidates walk predecessor links without allocating full paths. Education uses reachability checks rather than materializing paths. Only the bounded actual trip sample produces path arrays. Private-state report copies follow the strict known schema and preserve defensive copies of nested arrays/records.

This is a semantics-preserving refactor: evidence compares the preserved preoptimization module against the candidate over 96 synthetic city-months, including closure/fire/removal/rebuild, road-capacity, staffing, prices, goods, utility, household and schooling changes. Actual city state, receipts, reports, chosen paths and full traffic arrays matched exactly. The before/after dense fixture measurements are performance observations, not a new behavioral RED claim, and the 20 ms p95 target remains unmet on the recorded dense after-run. Details are in the ignored CMD-03 household performance evidence.
